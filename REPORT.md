Reading: 39 of 120 cells (32.5%) can be settled by inspecting a published evaluation. 75 (62.5%) can only be settled by organisational records that no publication carries. 6 (5.0%) are judgements that no artefact settles. A completed matrix therefore contains 81 cells (67.5%) whose traffic-light colour is, to anyone outside the organisation, an assertion.

## 3. The study set

PubMed was queried on the date recorded in `data/selection.json` with the query stored there. 145 records were returned and 145 screened. 74 met the four content criteria (primary evaluation, generative or fine-tuned LLM, title and abstract screening, a metric against a human reference); 51 of those also have open-access full text in PubMed Central. A random sample of 12 was drawn with seed 20260911. One further study was added by design because the Ada Lovelace Institute report alludes to it (GPT-4 across peer-reviewed and grey literature in multiple languages); its journal version is paywalled, so the arXiv version was used.
